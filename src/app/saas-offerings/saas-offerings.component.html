    Optimus: Auto Route Planning
  • Predictive solution for optimal vehicle and route planning, analyzing demand data to determine vehicle needs and create efficient delivery routes.
  • Plans routes for ~400+ delivery locations within a minute, saving an hour compared to manual planning.
  • Multiple configurations, including bulk weight and network effect, along with the option to input available vehicle types and numbers.
  • User-friendly UI/UX to modify proposed routes based on the actual situation on the ground.
    Smart Trip - Total Transport Management System
  • Vendor, Vehicle and Driver Onboarding: Supports seamless onboarding with validation using government APIs for GSTIN, banking, PAN, AADHAR, vehicle registration, and license details.
  • Trip Assignment and Tracking: Assign trips to vehicles and track them in real-time from start to final closure, including order delivery, proof of delivery, and COD collection.
  • Driver App: Designed for drivers, this tool ensures seamless deliveries with automated warehouse checks, route sequencing, material verification, real-time updates, and post-delivery processing.
  • Supervisor App: A command center for supervisors to manage delivery operations, including vehicle availability, trip assignment, real-time tracking, driver communication, and vendor registration.
    HiSaaB - Contract Management & Accounting Tool
  • A comprehensive commercial and contract management tool for teams handling e-contracts across transport facilities within companies.
  • Vendor Commercial Management: HiSaaB streamlines vendor contract management with easy oversight across projects, tracking based on various parameters, and comparison to master contracts.
  • Approval of Contracts based on User Management: HiSaaB enhances contract approval security and accountability by restricting access to authorized personnel and streamlining authentication processes.
  • Billing and Cost Allocation: HiSaaB streamlines invoicing processes and offers advanced cost analysis tools for comprehensive trip expense evaluation and optimization of cost allocation strategies.
